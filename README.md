# Jamie Jacklin
My journey into geospatial technology truly began after attending the 2026 Esri User Conference in San Diego. It was there that I discovered a profound parallel between building financial ledgers and building maps: both systems exist to structure complex backend data and translate it into a clear, actionable visual story.
I have been attending Riverside City College off-and-on for 25 years and I am a perpetual student.  I have been a Riverside County Employee for 18 years, two years ago I accepted a promotion at the Transportation Land Management Agency two years ago.  I attended the 2026 E.S.R.I. conference in San Diego, and became interested in G.I.S.  My background is in financial reporting and enjoyed maintaining different databases and learning how they worked together.  In learning about the G.I.S., I am interested in the similarities in building financials and maps to convey information.
